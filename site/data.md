The raw outputs of the framework's agent-based simulation: individual scenario runs, parameter sweeps and Monte Carlo distributions, as the files the simulation wrote. They come from the version 1.x and version 2.0 simulation and from the 2026 work that revalidated it.

## STATUS

These files are a record, not current findings. In September 2026 an audit found defects in some of the simulation's measurements, and some of the figures drawn from these files are withdrawn or under review. The instrument validation record lists each one, and the reason.

Dataset names describe each scenario as it was designed, not what it showed. "Drift Contained", for example, names a scenario in which a defense against slow drift was switched on. Whether drift can be contained is currently uncharacterized: neither shown contained nor shown uncontained.

The version 3 simulation is a rebuild, registered before it ran. Its results stay sealed until they have been reviewed, and will be published with their evidence.
