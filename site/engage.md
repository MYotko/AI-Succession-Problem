This framework is built in the open and designed to get stronger under scrutiny. If you have something to contribute, I want to hear from you.

## HOW TO ENGAGE

### 01 Challenge the framework
If you find a flaw in the equilibrium analysis, a gap in the consensus protocol's specification, an error in the simulation code or a limitation not yet identified, that is valuable. Open an issue on GitHub with the specifics. This is how the framework improves: in September 2026 the claim that cooperation is the unique equilibrium failed exactly this kind of scrutiny, and the correction is published.
[Open an issue](https://github.com/MYotko/AI-Succession-Problem/issues)

### 02 Extend the work
If you see a way to close a specification gap, resolve a constitutional question, connect the framework to your field, or add simulation coverage for an untested attack vector, the repository is open and contributions are welcome.
[View open problems](https://github.com/MYotko/AI-Succession-Problem/blob/main/docs/SPECIFICATION_GAPS.md)

### 03 Collaborate
If you are working on AI governance, game-theoretic approaches to alignment, constitutional design for AI systems, or related areas and see potential for collaboration, that conversation is welcome.
[Get in touch](mailto:yotko@lineageimperative.org)

### 04 General inquiries
For questions, feedback, speaking inquiries, or anything that doesn't fit the categories above.
[Send an email](mailto:yotko@lineageimperative.org)

## WHAT TO EXPECT

I read everything. I respond to substantive engagement. If you challenge a claim with specifics, you will get a specific response. If you identify an error, it will be corrected and documented in the version history, as every previous correction has been.

For technical issues, bug reports, or specific challenges to the framework's claims, GitHub issues are preferred. For everything else, email is fine.

> The framework is built in sustained collaboration with several frontier AI systems, put under deliberate pressure by an internal audit and blind, independent re-derivations, and revised when the evidence required it. That process continues, and your engagement is part of it.
