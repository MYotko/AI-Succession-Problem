How to cite the paper, the essays and the project's records.

## The Lineage Imperative (Framework Paper)

> The current version, v2.0, has been under major revision since 25 September 2026. Cite it as a working
> paper, but do not cite its formal results. The corrections are in the instrument validation record, below.
> Version 3 will replace it.

### BibTeX

```bibtex
@misc{yotko2026lineage,
  author       = {Yotko, Matthew},
  title        = {The {Lineage} {Imperative}: {A} Formal Governance Framework for Human-AI Coexistence},
  year         = {2026},
  month        = {June},
  howpublished = {Working paper, GitHub,
                  \url{https://github.com/MYotko/AI-Succession-Problem}},
  note         = {Version 2.0, under major revision since 25 September 2026; licensed CC BY 4.0},
}
```

### APA (7th edition)

Yotko, M. (2026, June). *The Lineage Imperative: A formal governance framework for human-AI coexistence* (Version 2.0) \[Working paper, under major revision\]. GitHub. https://github.com/MYotko/AI-Succession-Problem

### Plain Text

Matthew Yotko. "The Lineage Imperative: A Formal Governance Framework for Human-AI Coexistence." Version 2.0, working paper, June 2026; under major revision since 25 September 2026. https://github.com/MYotko/AI-Succession-Problem. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## The AI Succession Problem (Essay Series)

Ten essays, published on Substack at https://yotko.substack.com from March to May 2026. Use the series citation for the work as a whole, and an individual citation for a specific essay.

### Series: BibTeX

```bibtex
@misc{yotko2026aisuccession_series,
  author       = {Yotko, Matthew},
  title        = {The {AI} Succession Problem},
  year         = {2026},
  howpublished = {Essay series, Substack,
                  \url{https://yotko.substack.com}},
  note         = {Ten essays, published March to May 2026},
}
```

### Series: APA (7th edition)

Yotko, M. (2026). *The AI succession problem* \[Essay series\]. Substack. https://yotko.substack.com

### Series: Plain Text

Matthew Yotko. "The AI Succession Problem." Essay series. Substack, March to May 2026. https://yotko.substack.com
