# Ideas

This directory holds idea-level, hypothesis-only material. Nothing here is
validated, nothing here is evidentiary, and nothing here may be cited as
evidence for the framework or for any claim the framework makes. Documents in
this drawer record directions of approach that seemed worth preserving, not
results, and they should be read as open questions rather than as findings.
Because this material is speculative and would be misleading if it circulated
alongside validated work, the drawer is deliberately excluded from committed
snapshots by the never-ingest rule in
`scripts/generate_project_knowledge_snapshots.py`, and that exclusion is
independently verified by `scripts/check_snapshot_leak.py`. Promoting anything
from here means doing the work to validate it and moving the result out of this
directory.
